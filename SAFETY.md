# Safety and Privacy Rules

This project exists to make communities safer. These rules protect the people who use it and the people it talks about.

## Emergency numbers

- **10111**: SAPS emergency
- **112**: emergency from a cellphone
- **08600 10111**: Crime Stop (anonymous tip-offs)
- **0800 428 428**: GBV Command Centre (24 hours)

## The golden rules

1. **No personal information.** Do not post names, ID numbers, phone numbers, addresses, vehicle registrations or photos of any real person (victim, witness or suspect).
2. **No vigilantism.** Nothing here may encourage violence, intimidation or "street justice". Crime must be reported to the police.
3. **No unverified accusations.** Everyone is innocent until proven guilty in court. Do not publish rumours.
4. **Anonymise everything.** Data must be aggregated (for example by police precinct or ward) and must not allow anyone to be identified.
5. **Credit your sources.** Every dataset must link to where it came from and state its licence.
6. **Follow POPIA.** The Protection of Personal Information Act applies to anything involving personal data.

## For contributors

- Never commit passwords, API keys or private files. Use a `.gitignore` file.
- If you accidentally upload personal information, **tell a maintainer immediately** so it can be removed from the repository history.
- Treat sensitive topics (such as gender-based violence) with care. Link to professional support services.

## Reporting a problem

If you find content that puts someone at risk, open an Issue marked **"safety"** without repeating the sensitive details, or contact the maintainer directly.
