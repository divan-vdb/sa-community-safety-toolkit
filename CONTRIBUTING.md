# How to Contribute

Thank you for helping make South African communities safer! You do not need to be a programmer. Designers, writers, translators and researchers are all welcome.

## Ways to help

- **Write or improve guides** in `docs/` (plain language please)
- **Translate** guides into Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho and other South African languages
- **Add public datasets** to `data/` with clear sources
- **Build tools** such as dashboards, checklists and report templates
- **Fix typos** and improve the README

## Before you start

1. Read [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md). It is short and important.
2. Check the Issues tab to see whether someone is already working on your idea.

## How to submit a change

1. Click **Fork** (top right) to make your own copy
2. Make your changes
3. Click **Contribute** then **Open pull request**
4. Describe what you changed and why

A maintainer will review it. Be patient and kind. Everyone here is a volunteer.

## Standards

- Use clear, simple language
- Cite sources for every fact or dataset
- No personal information, ever
- Be respectful. Harassment and hate speech lead to removal from the project.
